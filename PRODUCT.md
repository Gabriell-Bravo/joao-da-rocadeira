# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user ("usando o motion"): React + Vite + Motion (motion/react), the same stack as the sibling project in the workspace. Static build, no backend.

## Users

People in the Região dos Lagos (RJ) planning a floor, a bathroom or a terrace: homeowners, small builders, reformers. They arrive from Instagram or WhatsApp on a phone, comparing prices and deciding whom to call. The job: see that the store is real, big and cheap, then message the right unit.

## Product Purpose

Brand site for João da Roçadeira, a store/fábrica in Saquarema and Araruama (RJ) that sells floors and porcelain tile ("Rei do Piso e Porcelanato", about 10 carretas per month), and also makes nautical-rope ("tricô náutico") furniture, plus whirlpool bathtubs and generators. Success is a WhatsApp conversation with the right unit.

## Positioning

Volume and price: sells directly, covers competitor quotes ("cubro orçamentos"), and is also the factory of its own nautical-rope furniture. Open since 1988 (stated on the logo).

## Operating Context

Sales happen on WhatsApp. Two units, each with its own number. Instagram (@joaodarocadeira, ~30.5K followers) is the main marketing channel, with reels of the stock and promotions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two WhatsApp lines: Saquarema +55 22 99830-6657, Araruama +55 22 99608-0318 (source: linktr.ee/JoaodaRocadeira).
- No prices, stock lists, addresses, opening hours or delivery terms were confirmed. None may be invented. Addresses and hours are undecided and must be marked as to be filled in.
- Floor-area calculator is an estimator only (area plus loss margin); it must not quote prices.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: yellow disc, black ring, black lettering "JOÃO da ROÇADEIRA — DESDE 1988" (`public/media/logo-150.jpg`, only 150px available from Instagram).
- Brand colours seen in the logo and story highlights: saturated yellow and black.
- Voice: direct, loud, Brazilian retail ("vendo 10 carretas por mês, tenho preço, cubro orçamentos").
- The political-discount reel (Bolsonaro/Lula) is excluded from the site.

## Evidence on Hand

- Instagram reels downloaded as video-only clips under `public/media/` (Black Friday, pisos e porcelanatos, tricô náutico factory, banheiras, geradores, mesas cascata).
- Instagram bio and caption copy. No customer testimonials, no press, no certifications. Do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. Show real stock and real reels before any claim.
2. One tap to WhatsApp, always, with the right unit.
3. Loud like the store, never like a template.
4. Say only what the Instagram and the store already say.
